Launch your non-profit site free on Google Workspace

A step-by-step path from a bare domain to a live, professional website — without paying for hosting. This guide assumes your organization already has a Google Workspace account.

Before you begin

You'll move faster if you gather these first:

  • Admin access to your organization's Google Workspace
  • Your domain name and access to its DNS settings
  • Your logo, a few photos, and the text for each page

The steps

1

Confirm your domain

Make sure the domain is verified inside your Workspace account so you can point it at your new site.

2

Prepare your content

Write the words for each page and collect your images. Clear, short copy beats long paragraphs.

3

Build and publish

Your site is built as fast static files and deployed to free hosting connected to your Workspace.

4

Wire up your contact form

Connect the form to a Google Sheet so submissions are logged and emailed to your team automatically.

5

Point your domain and go live

Update DNS to your new site. Within a short while, visitors see your live site at your own domain.

Quick checklist

  • Domain verified in Workspace
  • Page content and images ready
  • Contact form connected to a Sheet
  • DNS pointed at the live site
